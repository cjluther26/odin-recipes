"CJ Kibble"


Credit: iamafoodblog.com

Description

Sometimes, you just want a bowl of clean, nutritious food! This bowl is packed with slow-digesting carbohydrates, a lean protein, some healthy fats, and nutrient-rich vegetables. It's a great meal choice for anyone looking to build muscle or even just maintain a healthy lifestyle!

Ingredients

  • 6oz 96% lean ground beef
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 3 eggs
  • 26g beef tallow
  • A handful of steamed broccoli
  • Any zero-to-low calorie sauces you prefer (added liberally)

Steps

  1. Add cooking spray to a nonstick pan, heat it, and add the ground beef. About halfway through, add 3 eggs. Cook thoroughly.
  2. Add 2 cups of cooked rice to a bowl. This can be done in a multitude of ways: microwaved rice, pre-prepped rice from a rice cooker, etc.
  3. Add broccoli (or whichever vegetables you choose) to the bowl of rice. Microwave until hot.
  4. Add the beef tallow and beef/egg mix.
  5. Heat in microwave for ~2:00 (or until desired temperature).
  6. Add sauces and enjoy!

Additional Notes

  • Adjust to your caloric needs! If you need a fattier protein in this meal, maybe use 90/10 ground beef instead of 96/4! Perhaps you prefer 90/10 but don't want to add more fat to the meal...that's OK, too, just use less beef tallow! Cater any ingredient here to your individual caloric needs. Remember, we all have different ways of eating, metabolisms, and needs at every meal!
