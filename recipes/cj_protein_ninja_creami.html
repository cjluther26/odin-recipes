CJ's Protein Ninja Creami


Credit: lovegrowswild.com

Description

Craving ice cream but want to keep the overall caloric profile a little lower and the protein higher? So long as you have a Ninja Creami machine, this recipe will scratch that itch! It's super-simple to prep and easily-customizable to meet any caloric needs.

Just be sure to make it ahead of time -- the mixture needs to freeze for ~24 hours (according to Ninja) before its ready to run through the machine.

Ingredients

  • 1 ready-to-drink (RTD) protein shake
  • 2 teaspoons of guar gum
  • 3 packets stevia / calore-free sweetener
  • A pinch (or two) of pink himalayan sea salt
  • A splash of unsweetened almond milk

Steps

  1. Pour the RTD into an empty Ninja Creami container.
  2. Add your chosen sweetener, salt, and guar gum. Stir thoroughly!
  3. Add the lid and place it in the freezer (ensuring it's level there.)
  4. Let it set in the freezer for ~24 hours.
  5. Let's run it! Fire up the Ninja Creami machine, adding the container and running it on the following settings: Full | Lite. Add a splash of almond milk and re-spin, still on Lite, but this time, using either the "Full" or "Bottom" setting.
  6. Optional: you may have to continue to re-spin until desired consistency is met (I usually have to do an additional run). Feel free to continue until you're satisfied.

Additional Notes

  • Please customize your flavors / toppings! Use whatever RTD you like...you can even use a cup of almond milk and a scoop of your favorite protein powder instead! If you'd like to add toppings, do so as desired, using the "Toppings" setting on the Ninja Creami machine.
  • Adjust to your caloric needs! If you need additional calories here, feel free to add toppings as you see fit.
